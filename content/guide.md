## Other practical aspects

Before teaching the lesson, instructors should decide how far to go into the
attention calculation. The important distinction is between:

- attention, which exchanges information between token positions;
- feed-forward layers, which transform each token representation;
- the training objective, which adjusts both components through examples.

The lesson should avoid presenting attention heads as hand-designed
linguistic rules. Examples involving nouns, adjectives, or pronouns are useful
illustrations, but the actual features learned by a model are distributed and
often difficult to interpret directly.
